Our Story

Baked with Passion,
Served with Pride

Founded in the heart of the city, Maison Du Beurre has been crafting artisan breads and pastries for over a decade. We source the finest organic grains and use traditional techniques to bring you bread the way it was meant to be.

From our sourdough starter that's been alive for 10 years, to our buttery croissants laminated by hand — every product is a labour of love.
